# IPTV Player: privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

IPTV Player is a video player for Android TV. It comes with no channels or content: it plays only the
playlists that you add.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. The
developer receives no data from it.

## What stays on your device

- The playlists you add: their names, M3U links, and Xtream Codes server addresses, usernames and passwords.
- Your favourites, and the point where you stopped each movie or episode.

These are kept in the app's private storage on your TV. They are excluded from Android backups, so they
are not copied to a cloud backup or to another device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in the
TV's settings, deletes them.

## Network connections

The app connects only to the servers you enter, to load playlists, channel logos, posters and video
streams. Those servers belong to your IPTV provider, not to the developer, and their own privacy policies
apply to what they receive (for example your IP address and your login).

## Permissions

`INTERNET`, to reach the servers you add. No other permission is requested.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
